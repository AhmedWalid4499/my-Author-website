The story

Inside the book

Every morning, Hikari chases the perfect sunrise. Every morning, it eludes her. Then one evening, a photograph falls from the sky — already developed, already perfect, and dated tomorrow.

It is signed by someone named Kai. Someone who knows her name. Someone who hasn’t arrived yet.

As a mysterious fog tightens its grip on her coastal town, the photographs keep coming — each one more urgent than the last. Hikari doesn’t know who Kai is. But she is beginning to suspect that finding him before the next sunrise matters more than she could have imagined.

Some messages travel across time. Some sunrises change everything.

The Perfect Sunrise is about memory, loss, and the quiet truth that the people we love never truly leave us — even when we can no longer see them.
