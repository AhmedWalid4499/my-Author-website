What are you sending?

Two kinds. The advance copy is the themed letter with the EPUB attached; its words live in tools/arc/template.mjs. A letter is anything else — you write it here.

Blank line starts a paragraph. {{first}} their first name, {{name}} their full one. *italic*, **bold**, [words](https://…), # heading, > quoted, --- a divider.

People

      
Your list, kept in tools/admin/contacts.csv — you can open
         that in Numbers instead if you prefer. Groups are free text: put
         arc on your advance readers, press on reviewers, whatever
         you like, separated by semicolons. Nothing is collected from the website; this list
         is only what you put in it.

Paste in a lot at once

      
One per line. Name <name@example.com>, or
         Name, name@example.com, or just the address. They are added to the
         groups ticked below, and anyone already on the list is left alone.

Your Gmail

          
Gmail needs an app password, not your normal one:
             Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords. It is held
             in memory for the length of one send and never written down.
             Gmail also stops a normal account after about 500 messages in a day.
