Before you write

What I love to hear

Email is the surest way to reach me. A few things that are always welcome in the inbox:

  • What a story meant to you. Which scene stayed, which character you saw yourself in, which line you read twice — this is the best kind of letter there is.
  • Questions about the books. Where an idea came from, what a title says in Japanese, or which of the three to start with.
  • Press and interview requests. The covers and ready-to-use bios are already gathered in the press kit — take whatever you need, and write if something is missing.

Notes in Japanese are welcome too. I am still learning the language, so I will read them slowly — and gladly.
