About Me

I am an Egyptian boy who had always dreamt of magic — the kind I'd always wished I could show everyone.

By day, I work as a Project Manager and assistant lecturer at university. In between, I write books about train stations and cafés and sunrises, and I build websites that try to feel like places worth visiting.

I grew up lost in books — Rick Riordan's mythologies, Brandon Sanderson's worlds, Cassandra Clare's shadowhunters. They were the ones who made me believe that stories could do something real to a person. They made me want to write.

I am also slowly, happily learning Japanese — one word at a time, one sentence at a time, one story at a time.
