Which one first

There isn’t a reading order

None of these is a sequel to any of the others. Nothing carries over, nothing is set up, and no book needs another one to make sense of it. Start with whichever cover keeps pulling your eye back.

If you would rather be pointed somewhere, here is each of them in a line:

  • From Tomorrow — a train line closing in three days, and two strangers passing notes across a day neither of them has lived yet.
  • Sakura Slice Café — the quieter door. Thirteen short stories you can take one at a time, whenever you want somewhere warm to sit for a while.
  • The Perfect Sunrise — a photograph that arrives before the day it shows.
  • When You Were There — a cassette tape that should not exist.
  • The Blue Bookmark Bookshop — a bookshop that wasn’t there yesterday and won’t be there tomorrow. Still coming.

The reading list below is where the next one gets announced first, and Goodreads is where it becomes real to everyone else.
