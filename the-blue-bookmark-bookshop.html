What there is to tell so far

The shop

There is a chalkboard outside, and it has said the same thing since the day the shop opened.

And by the door there is an umbrella stand, with a smaller sign on it that asks for one thing only.

That is most of what I can tell you yet. The rest is the book, and the book is nearly finished.
