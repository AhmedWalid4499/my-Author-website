Full

154 words

Ahmed El-Bourgy is an author from Cairo, Egypt, who describes himself as an Egyptian boy who had always dreamt of magic, the kind he had always wished he could show everyone. He writes quiet, Japanese-inspired fiction about the magic hiding in ordinary moments: train stations, café tables, and the space between one day and the next. From Tomorrow follows two strangers exchanging notes dated a day they have not yet lived; Sakura Slice Café collects thirteen quiet stories from a café that was never just a café; The Perfect Sunrise follows a girl chasing a sunrise that keeps eluding her until a photograph arrives already dated tomorrow; and When You Were There begins with a grandmother’s cassette tape and the voice a boy finds on it. All four are available on Kindle. Away from the page, Ahmed works as a Project Manager and an assistant lecturer at university, builds websites, and is learning Japanese.
